Dalil

Dalil is a private assistant used by Laraj Group, a real estate practice in Cary, North Carolina. It reads the practice's own business email and calendar to prepare a daily summary and to draft replies for the agent to review.

Dalil is not a public service. Only accounts belonging to Laraj Group can sign in. Nothing is sent without the agent's approval.
