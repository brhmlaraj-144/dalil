Dalil privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026.

Dalil is a private assistant operated by Laraj Group for its own use. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

Who can use it

Only Google accounts that belong to Laraj Group and that its operator has added. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the account owner's consent, Dalil reads Gmail messages and Google Calendar events in that account, and can send an email reply from that account after the owner approves the exact text.

How the data is used

To write a daily summary for the account owner, to draft replies for the owner to review, and to keep notes on the owner's own business contacts and deals. Message content is processed by an AI model provider (Anthropic) solely to produce those summaries and drafts.

What it does not do

Dalil does not sell data, does not share it with advertisers, does not use it to train AI models, and does not transfer it to anyone except the service providers needed to run the assistant. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Notes and summaries are stored on a computer controlled by the operator. The account owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and can ask for stored notes to be deleted.
